Fire

class Fire : public minim::NewMinimiser<Fire>

FIRE (Fast Inertial Relaxation Engine) minimisation.

A damped molecular dynamics method: velocities follow the forces, with an adaptive time step that grows during downhill motion and resets when the motion turns uphill. Converged when the RMS gradient falls below the state’s convergence criterion.
