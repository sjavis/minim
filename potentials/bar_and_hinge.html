BarAndHinge

class BarAndHinge : public minim::NewPotential<BarAndHinge>

The bar-and-hinge model for thin sheet buckling.

Represents an elastic sheet as a triangulated surface: bars (edges) stretch with energy \( k (l - l_0)^2 / 2 \), and hinges (pairs of adjacent triangles) bend with energy \( k (\theta - \theta_0)^2 / 2 \). Optionally includes substrate and external force interactions.

The bar and hinge lists are generated from a triangulation, or can be given explicitly. Rest lengths and angles are taken from the initial coordinates unless set.
