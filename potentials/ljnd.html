Lennard-Jones

LjNd

class LjNd : public minim::NewPotential<LjNd>

Lennard-Jones potential in an arbitrary number of dimensions.

Pairwise interaction of particles, with energy \( 4 \epsilon \left[ (\sigma/r)^{12} - (\sigma/r)^{6} \right] \), where \( r \) is the particle separation. The repulsive \( r^{-12} \) and attractive \( r^{-6} \) terms give a potential well of depth \( \epsilon \) at separation \( \sigma 2^{1/6} \).

The number of particles is inferred from the length of the coordinates, and all pairs are used as energy elements.

Subclassed by minim::Lj2d, minim::Lj3d
